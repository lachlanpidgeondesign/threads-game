How to Play

Some squares have wet ink. Fold the paper along a line and press: the squares that touch print onto each other, both ways, then the paper opens back up.

Tap a tab along the top or left edge to pick a fold and peek: the paper lifts, and arcs join each pair of squares that will touch, in the colour they'll become. Slide along the edge to try the lines beside it. Peeking is free.

Tap the paper to press it, or drag the tab across the paper to fold it shut.

Every press counts, even ones you undo, so try to match par. Ink can't be wiped off, so if it lands where it shouldn't, Undo.
