How to Play

Some squares have wet ink. Fold the paper along a line and press: everything that touches prints onto the other side, both ways, then the paper opens back up.

Print the picture in as few presses as you can. Pick up a side and fold it over a line: once it's past upright you'll see what each touching pair becomes, and letting go presses it. Or tap a notch and then the sheet. Ink can't be wiped off, so if it lands where it shouldn't, just Undo.
